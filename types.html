02 / THE FORMS

Different types of bullying.

Bullying can take many forms. Sometimes it is obvious, and sometimes it is quiet and difficult to notice.

✦
💡

Recognising the type helps us understand what is happening and choose a safer way to respond.

Four common forms

01

VERBAL

Insults, cruel jokes, threats, name-calling or comments meant to humiliate.

✦
02

SOCIAL

Leaving someone out on purpose, spreading rumours or turning friends against them.

✦
03

PHYSICAL

Hitting, pushing, tripping, damaging belongings or unwanted physical contact.

✦
04

CYBERBULLYING

Using messages, social media, games or websites to threaten, embarrass or harass someone.

✦
💛
Remember this

Being kind does not mean staying silent when something is wrong. You can be respectful and still set clear boundaries.
