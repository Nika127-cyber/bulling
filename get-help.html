05 / YOU ARE NOT ALONE

You deserve support.

If bullying is happening to you or someone you know, telling a trusted person is a strong first step. You are not responsible for someone else's choice to hurt you.

✦
💡

Choose an adult who listens and takes you seriously. This could be a parent, relative, teacher, school counsellor, coach or another safe adult.

Words you can use

01

START THE CONVERSATION

“I need to tell you something that has been happening. I feel worried and I need your help.”

✦
02

EXPLAIN WHAT HAPPENED

“This has happened more than once. It usually happens at school / online / after class.”

✦
03

ASK FOR A PLAN

“Can we work out what to do next and how to keep me safe?”

✦
04

IF SOMEONE IS IN IMMEDIATE DANGER

Move to a safer place and contact a trusted adult or local emergency services. Do not confront a dangerous person alone.

✦
💛
Remember this

Being kind does not mean staying silent when something is wrong. You can be respectful and still set clear boundaries.
