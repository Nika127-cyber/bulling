How can we stop bullying?

Stopping bullying is a shared responsibility. You do not need to solve it alone, and you should never put yourself in danger to intervene.

✦
💡

A safe response can be simple: support the person, report what happened, keep useful evidence and involve a trusted adult.

A practical action plan

01

SUPPORT

Listen without blaming. Say: “I’m glad you told me. You don’t deserve this.”

✦
02

REPORT

Tell a teacher, school counsellor, parent or another trusted adult. Be clear about what happened.

✦
03

SAVE EVIDENCE

For online bullying, keep screenshots, dates and usernames. Do not forward hurtful content.

✦
04

FOLLOW UP

Check in later. If the first adult does not help, tell another trusted adult.

✦
💛
Remember this

Being kind does not mean staying silent when something is wrong. You can be respectful and still set clear boundaries.
