03 / THE IMPACT

The effects of bullying.

Bullying can affect a person's feelings, body, relationships and ability to learn. Everyone reacts differently, and no one deserves to be treated this way.

✦
💡

The effects are not always visible. A person may laugh or say they are fine while privately feeling worried or unsafe.

What someone might experience

01

EMOTIONAL

Fear, sadness, loneliness, shame, anger or lower self-confidence.

✦
02

SCHOOL & LEARNING

Trouble concentrating, missing school, falling behind or avoiding activities.

✦
03

PHYSICAL

Headaches, stomach aches, sleep problems or feeling tense and exhausted.

✦
04

SOCIAL

Losing trust, withdrawing from friends or finding it harder to feel safe around others.

✦
💛
Remember this

Being kind does not mean staying silent when something is wrong. You can be respectful and still set clear boundaries.
