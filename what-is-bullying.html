01 / THE BASICS

What is bullying?

Bullying is repeated, intentional behaviour that hurts, intimidates or excludes another person. It can happen in person or online.

✦
💡

A disagreement is not always bullying. Look for a pattern of harmful behaviour, an intention to hurt, and a power imbalance that makes it difficult for the targeted person to defend themselves.

The three key signs

01

REPEATED

It happens more than once, or there is a real risk it will happen again.

✦
02

INTENTIONAL HARM

The behaviour is meant to hurt, frighten, humiliate or exclude.

✦
03

POWER IMBALANCE

One person or group has more social, physical or digital power.

✦
💛
Remember this

Being kind does not mean staying silent when something is wrong. You can be respectful and still set clear boundaries.
